How It Works

CardSense is designed to make managing your credit cards straightforward, secure, and hassle-free.

1. Add your cards

Start by adding your credit cards to the app. You don't need to link your bank account or provide any login credentials.

2. Import your statements

When you receive your monthly Statement of Account (SOA), simply import the PDF into CardSense. The app processes the file locally on your device to extract your due dates, total amounts, and transactions.

3. Track and organize

CardSense gives you a clear dashboard showing all your upcoming due dates, recent spending across all cards, and rewards tracking—so you never miss a payment and always maximize your benefits.

4. Stay secure

Because everything happens on your device, your financial data stays private. We don't have access to your bank account, and your statement files are never uploaded to the cloud.
