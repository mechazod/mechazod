# CardSense – Credit Card Tracker

**Your Philippine credit cards, organized.**

CardSense is a credit-card tracker designed for Filipino cardholders. Keep your cards, statements, due dates, rewards, and spending information organized in one place.

🔒 **Privacy-first by design**
CardSense is designed to minimize unnecessary access to your financial information.
You don't need to give CardSense your online-banking password or bank login credentials.
For supported PDF statements, CardSense is designed to process the statement locally on your device.

💳 **Manage your cards**
Keep your credit cards organized in one place instead of relying on spreadsheets, notes, or memory.

📄 **Import supported statements**
Import supported credit-card PDF statements and let CardSense help organize the information contained in them.

📅 **Track important dates**
Keep track of statement dates, payment due dates, and other important card information.

🎁 **Keep an eye on rewards**
Organize rewards information so you can better understand which cards you're using and what benefits they offer.

📊 **Understand your spending**
Review your card activity and organize your spending information in one place.

🇵🇭 **Built for Filipino cardholders**
CardSense is designed around the way Filipino cardholders manage Philippine credit cards and statements.

**CardSense does NOT need your bank password**
Your bank credentials, OTPs, PINs, and authentication codes should remain private.
CardSense does not need them to organize your credit-card information.

**Important**
CardSense is a credit-card organization and tracking tool. It is not a bank, lender, payment service, or credit-card issuer.
CardSense does not make payments or transfer money from your bank account.
Always use your bank's official channels for banking transactions and payments.
