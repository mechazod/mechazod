CardSense FAQ

What is CardSense?

CardSense is a credit-card organization app designed for Filipino cardholders.

It helps you keep track of your cards, statements, payment due dates, rewards, spending, and other important credit-card information in one place.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. CardSense is designed to work without requiring your online-banking username or password.

You can enter information yourself and, where supported, import your credit-card statement.

Does CardSense need my bank password?

No.

You should never give your bank password, OTP, PIN, or security code to CardSense or to anyone claiming to represent CardSense.

Does CardSense upload my credit-card statement?

CardSense is designed to process supported PDF statements locally on your device.

The purpose is to let you organize information from your statement without requiring the statement itself to be uploaded to a CardSense server.

What happens when I import a statement?

CardSense reads the information contained in a supported statement and uses it to help organize your credit-card information inside the app.

Your original statement remains under your control.

What banks does CardSense support?

CardSense is designed for Philippine credit-card statements. Supported statement formats may vary by bank and can change when banks change their statement layouts.

If your statement is not recognized correctly, please contact us and provide the relevant details without sharing passwords, OTPs, PINs, or other authentication information.

Can I use CardSense with multiple credit cards?

Yes. CardSense is designed for people who manage multiple credit cards and want their information organized in one place.

Does CardSense make payments for me?

No.

CardSense helps you track and organize your credit-card information. It does not transfer money or pay your credit-card bill.

Use your bank's official payment channels to make payments.

Does CardSense replace my bank's app?

No.

CardSense is a companion organization tool. Your bank's official app or website remains the place to manage your bank account, make payments, view official account information, and perform banking transactions.

Is CardSense a bank?

No.

CardSense is not a bank, lender, payment provider, or credit-card issuer.

Does CardSense store my financial information?

CardSense is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of financial information and to process supported statements locally on your device.

The information you enter or create inside the app may be stored on your device so that the app can provide its features.

For the complete details, see our Privacy & Security page.

Does CardSense sell my financial information?

No. CardSense is designed so that your financial information is not sold to advertisers.

Is CardSense free?

CardSense offers a free experience, with additional features available depending on the current plan.

Please check the current CardSense pricing page for the latest details.

Can I delete my information?

Your ability to delete information depends on where that information is stored and which version of CardSense you are using.

For locally stored information, deleting the relevant data from the app or removing the app can affect information stored on your device.

For specific deletion requests or questions, contact CardSense support.

Is CardSense secure?

CardSense is designed with privacy and local processing in mind.

No software can honestly guarantee absolute security. We therefore focus on reducing unnecessary exposure of sensitive financial information and continuously improving the app's security and privacy practices.

What should I never give CardSense?

Never provide your:

  • Bank password
  • OTP
  • Card PIN
  • Online-banking security credentials
  • Authentication codes

CardSense does not need these credentials to organize your credit-card information.

What if I find a security problem?

Please report suspected security or privacy issues directly through the official CardSense support channel.

Please do not publicly post sensitive information, account details, passwords, OTPs, or screenshots containing personal financial information.

Is CardSense available for Android?

CardSense is available for Android.

Check the official Google Play listing for the current supported Android version and availability.

Who is CardSense for?

CardSense is especially useful for Filipino cardholders who have multiple cards, manage different statement and due dates, or want a simpler way to organize their credit-card information.

Does CardSense give financial advice?

CardSense is primarily an organization and tracking tool.

Information displayed by the app should not be treated as professional financial, legal, or tax advice. You remain responsible for decisions involving your credit cards and finances.
