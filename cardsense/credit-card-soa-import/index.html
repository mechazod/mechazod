Credit Card SOA Import

Dealing with multiple credit card Statement of Account (SOA) PDFs can be a hassle. CardSense simplifies this by letting you import your SOA directly into the app.

Offline-First & Secure

When you import your SOA, the parsing and data extraction happen locally on your device. Your sensitive financial documents are never uploaded to our servers.

How to import your SOA

1. Download your credit card SOA PDF from your email or bank portal.

2. Open CardSense and navigate to the import section.

3. Select the PDF file.

4. CardSense instantly reads the statement date, due date, transactions, and rewards.

Supported Banks

CardSense supports the majority of major Philippine banks. Our parsing engine is constantly updated to handle the various layout changes that banks periodically make to their statements.
