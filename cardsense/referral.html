Getting Started

How to invite a friend

From opening the referral screen to your friend's first sign-up, here's the full path.

1

Open the Referral Program

Go to More → Referral Program in the app. Available once you're on the Basic plan.

2

Get your unique code & link

CardSense generates a personal code (e.g. JOHN-A3B2) and a shareable link tied only to your account.

3

Share it with friends

Tap Share to send your link through Messenger, SMS, email, or any app — or hand them your code directly.

4

Friend installs & signs in

Your friend taps your link (or enters your code), installs CardSense, and signs in with Google on their own device.

5

Your progress updates automatically

Once they sign up — and later subscribe — your badge, progress bar, and rewards update in real time on the Referral screen.

6

Claim your reward

When a level unlocks, tap Activate Reward to apply your free months whenever you're ready.

Fair Play

Built to stop abuse, not friends

The referral program is monitored server-side to keep rewards fair for everyone. Here's what's protected.

🚫

Self-referral blocking

Your own email and referral code are automatically rejected — both on-device and on our servers — before a signup is ever recorded.

📵

Same-device detection

A secure device fingerprint checks that the referrer and referee aren't signing up from the same physical phone.

1️⃣

One referral per device

Each physical device can only ever be credited as a referee once — switching Google accounts or clearing app data doesn't reset it.

🛡️

Server-verified rewards

All signups, subscriptions, and reward grants are validated in our backend — never trusted from the app alone.

🚩

Fraud logging & auto-deactivation

Suspicious attempts are logged, and accounts that accumulate repeated red flags have their referral program automatically deactivated.

🔒

Privacy-masked emails

Referee emails are stored securely and only ever shown to you in masked form — never in full.

These protections run quietly in the background — legitimate referrals to real friends on their own devices are never affected.
