⚠️ The "Minimum Due" Trap

On a ₱25,000 balance at 3% monthly interest (36% p.a.), an ₱800 minimum payment pays ₱750 in interest and only ₱50 toward your actual balance. You could be paying for a decade without denting what you owe.

Payoff Journey changes this: It analyzes all your Philippine cards as one unified debt, safeguards every card against penalty fees, and directs extra funds where they do the most damage.

Two Proven Strategies. Instant Math.

⚡ Avalanche Method
Mathematically Optimal

Highest interest rate card first.

Pay minimums on all other cards to avoid penalties, and throw all excess budget at your highest APR balance.

Saves the absolute most money in total interest charges.

⛄ Snowball Method
Psychological Momentum

Smallest balance card first.

Pay minimums on everything else and knock out your smallest balances one by one for fast momentum.

Clears individual cards sooner to give you immediate breathing room.
